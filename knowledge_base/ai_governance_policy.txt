ENTERPRISE AI GOVERNANCE POLICY

This document defines organizational policy for the responsible use of
artificial intelligence systems.

1. DATA PRIVACY POLICY
All AI systems that process personal or customer data must comply with
applicable data protection regulations. Personal data used for AI training
or inference must be anonymized or pseudonymized wherever technically
feasible. Data retention for AI training datasets is limited to 24 months
unless a longer period is required by law.

2. HUMAN OVERSIGHT POLICY
Any AI-generated decision that materially affects a customer's financial
standing, employment status, or legal rights must be reviewed by a human
before being finalized. Fully automated decision-making is prohibited in
these categories.

3. MODEL RISK AND BIAS POLICY
All AI models must undergo a bias and fairness assessment before deployment
to production. Models must be re-evaluated for bias at least once every 12
months, or immediately after any significant retraining.

4. SECURITY POLICY
AI systems must be deployed within the organization's approved security
perimeter. Any AI system with access to production data must undergo a
security review prior to launch, and access must follow the principle of
least privilege.

5. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE POLICY
Teams deploying AI systems in regulated domains (finance, healthcare, HR)
must consult the Compliance team before deployment and maintain
documentation demonstrating adherence to relevant regulatory requirements.

6. VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY AI POLICY
Any third-party AI vendor or tool must be reviewed and approved by IT
Security and Legal before being integrated into company systems or given
access to company data.
